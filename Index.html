About me

I turn your ideas, notes and conversations into finished writing that sounds like you. You bring the expertise and the opinions. I bring the structure, the sentences and the deadline discipline.

You are the author. Your name goes on the work, I stay unnamed, and everything we discuss stays confidential.

How it works

  1. We talk

    A short call or voice note where you tell me what you want to say, who it is for and how you sound.

  2. I draft

    I write the piece in your voice and send it back on the agreed date.

  3. You review

    You comment, I revise, and we repeat until it reads exactly as you would say it.

  4. You publish

    The piece goes out under your name.
